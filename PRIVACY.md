PRIVACY POLICY — MoveYourAssets

Last updated: June 1, 2026

MoveYourAssets ("the App") is designed with your privacy in mind.

1. DATA COLLECTION
We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal data to external servers. All workout data (GPS tracks, distance, duration, heart rate) is stored exclusively on your device.

2. APPLE HEALTH
The App integrates with Apple Health to read and write workout data. This connection is optional and controlled entirely by you through iOS Settings. We do not access Apple Health data without your explicit permission.

3. LOCATION DATA
GPS location is used solely for real-time workout tracking. Location data is stored locally on your device and is never shared with third parties.

4. WEATHER DATA
The App uses the Open-Meteo API to display weather forecasts. Only your approximate coordinates are sent to retrieve weather data. No personal information is included in these requests.

5. ANALYTICS & ADVERTISING
We do not use any analytics, tracking, or advertising frameworks. There are no cookies, no user profiles, no behavioral tracking.

6. DATA STORAGE
All data is stored on-device using SwiftData and is included in your standard iOS/iCloud backup. We have no access to your data.

7. THIRD-PARTY SERVICES
- Open-Meteo (weather data): open-meteo.com/en/terms
- Apple HealthKit: subject to Apple's privacy policy

8. CHILDREN'S PRIVACY
The App does not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

9. CHANGES
We may update this policy. Changes will be reflected in the "Last updated" date above.
